Meet Our Strength Coach: Lance Channing 💪

Every week, personal trainer Lance Channing, who is also the Head Coach of our Women's A-Team, works with our athletes to build strength, agility and endurance.

Our goal is simple: raise healthy, competitive players who are ready for every game and for life beyond the court. Together with our mental coaching, this is how Black Star helps its players become the best version of themselves.

Strong bodies, strong minds, one family. 🖤⭐️
